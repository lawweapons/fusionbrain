**Operation flow rationale:** Face → rough OD to near-net → finish OD to nominal → thread → finish OD again at +0.001" offset → re-thread. The duplicated finish+thread sequence is the "one thou steps" method: the operator runs Ops 5–6 iteratively (each time with a 0.001" larger OD) to sneak up on a perfect thread fit for a specific mating part (e.g., suppressor mount).

## Patterns & Gotchas

- **"One thou steps" = iterative diameter creep, not a single-shot program.** Ops 5 and 6 are duplicates of Ops 3 and 4 with a deliberate +0.001" OD offset applied in the model or WCS shift. The document name ("v6") implies this has been run and re-run multiple times. Do not assume Op 5 is redundant — it is the fit-tuning mechanism.
- **Cutter comp changes between roughing and finishing.** Roughing (Op 2) uses `comp=control` (machine-side radius compensation); finishing (Ops 3, 5) uses `comp=computer` (Fusion-computed). Do not swap these — control-side comp on a finishing pass will behave differently depending on the controller's comp register state.
- **The stepover formula for profiling resolves to 1.0" in the digest** (`max(1.5 × 0.008", 1.0")`). This is a Fusion default formula artifact for turning — the actual radial depth of cut per roughing pass is controlled by the profile geometry, not this value literally. Verify actual DOC in simulation before running.
- **500 RPM is fixed for all ops including threading.** This is a deliberate conservative choice — likely driven by threading synchronization requirements or material/machine constraints. Do not increase RPM on the threading ops without verifying spindle encoder sync capability.
- **Thread pitch 0.041666" = 1/24" exactly (24 TPI).** The slight decimal imprecision is a floating-point artifact. The lathe controller will interpret this as 24 TPI; confirm the post-processor outputs a clean G32/G76 pitch value.
- **No parting or cutoff operation is present.** Either the part is cut off manually, or this program assumes the part is already at near-final length before facing. The face op (Op 1) sets the Z datum — confirm stock length allowance before running.

## How to Adapt This for a New Part

- [ ] **Confirm thread spec:** Pitch 0.041666" is hard-coded for 24 TPI. For any other thread (e.g., 5/8-28, M18×1), recalculate pitch and update Op 4 and Op 6.
- [ ] **Confirm OD diameter:** T1 (0.375" insert) is appropriate for 5/8" OD work. For significantly larger diameters, verify insert reach and corner radius are still suitable.
- [ ] **Verify 500 RPM is appropriate for your material.** At 5/8" dia, 500 RPM ≈ 82 SFM — conservative for steel/stainless, reasonable for harder alloys. Recalculate if material changes.
- [ ] **Replicate the "one thou steps" pairs only if fit-sneaking is needed.** For a standard production thread to a fixed tolerance, Ops 5–6 can be deleted and Op 3–4 run to final dimension directly.
- [ ] **Cutter comp mode:** If your controller handles comp differently, audit the `comp=control` (Op 2) vs. `comp=computer` (Ops 3, 5) split before posting.
- [ ] **Stock setup:** `offsetMode=simple` auto-sizes stock. If your blank has a specific pre-turned OD or non-standard length, override stock manually in Setup2 rather than relying on bounding-box auto-sizing.
- [ ] **T2 threading insert:** Described as "2.5mm" — verify this insert geometry matches the 60° thread form required for 5/8-24 UNF. The 2.5mm designation may refer to insert width, not thread form angle.
